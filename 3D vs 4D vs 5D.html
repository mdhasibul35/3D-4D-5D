Try this: drag the build slider to 0. Each shape collapses into the one before it: the cube flattens into a square, the tesseract into a cube, the penteract into a tesseract. Slide it back up and you watch each one get built by dragging a copy along a brand-new direction. That is the whole pattern, and it never stops: every new dimension doubles the corners.
